### 1a. 🔴 CRITICAL (NEW TODAY) — LITRG: 111,000 Unrepresented Taxpayers Still Unregistered

LITRG published a press release (sourced via litrg.org.uk and tax.org.uk) revealing that approximately **111,000 unrepresented taxpayers** — representing **52% of the 216,000 unrepresented MTD taxpayers HMRC identified** — have still not registered for Making Tax Digital. These taxpayers are now being auto-enrolled by HMRC using the process announced in August 2026 and documented in LITRG's updated guidance (24 September 2026).

AccountingWeb has already picked up the story with a piece titled *"MTD for IT — not going so well?"*, signalling active media interest in the registration failure narrative.

**Why this matters:**
- The "I haven't registered yet — what happens now?" query is now the largest single unserved audience in the MTD ITSA space
- 111,000 is a larger audience than the ~64,000 confirmed missed-Q1 filers and rivals the total 436,000 who have submitted
- The auto-enrolment letter creates a new, urgent moment: recipients will search for what HMRC's letter means and what they need to do
- None of STG's current 48 posts addresses auto-enrolment letters or the "what if I still haven't registered?" scenario
- This audience is also the Phase 2 (£30k–£50k) advance-warning audience — overlapping with the post identified as a gap in previous reports

**Content angle that no competitor has yet published:**  
*"I've Received an HMRC MTD Auto-Enrolment Letter — What Do I Do Now?"*  
Key differentiators: explains the auto-enrolment process, what the letter means, what taxpayers need to do, links to eligibility checker (to confirm they are actually in scope), links to software chooser (next step after confirmation). LITRG notes that many recipients may have low/modest incomes — some may not actually be in scope.

This post serves a distinct audience from the "missed Q1" reactive post and should not replace it — both are needed.
