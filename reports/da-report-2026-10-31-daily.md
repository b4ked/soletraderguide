**This is now confirmed.** Yesterday's report flagged this as "verify before updating." Research today confirms:

- HMRC raised the Approved Mileage Allowance Payment (AMAP) rate for cars and vans from **45p to 55p per mile** for the first 10,000 business miles per year.
- The rate applies from **6 April 2026** (2026/27 tax year) and was backdated to cover the full year including journeys completed before the announcement.
- HMRC announced the increase in **May 2026**, and the CIPP confirmed on **27 October 2026** that the Chancellor announced at the Autumn Budget that the higher rate is now **permanent**.
- The above-10,000-miles rate remains 25p. Motorcycles remain 24p. Bicycles remain 20p.
